What to measure

Real outcomes matter more than impressions: qualified enquiries, replies, meetings, and paying customers. These charts use only records that you have entered in this browser.

Your data stays here

This offline edition stores enquiries in this browser's local storage, without accounts, encryption or synchronisation. Do not use shared devices or store sensitive client data in this prototype. Browser clearing may erase records.

Control & safety

Drafts are suggestions, not sent messages. This app has no Gmail, LinkedIn or WhatsApp integration and never performs automated outreach. You must verify each person's context and comply with applicable data-protection and platform rules.

Optional AI handoff

For an AI-generated rewrite, select an enquiry and use “Copy AI brief”. Paste it into ChatGPT yourself, after checking that sharing its contents is appropriate. The built-in prioritisation and starter drafts are transparent rules and templates, not a generative AI service.
